Loadstone — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Loadstone is a pin-and-follow-back navigation app. It is built to keep your data on your device. This policy explains what the app accesses and why.

What Loadstone accesses

  • Location — used only while the app is open, to save a pin at your current spot and to point you back to a saved pin. Loadstone does not access your location in the background and does not track your movement.
  • Camera — used only when you choose to add a photo to a pin. Photos are saved in the app's private storage on your device.

What Loadstone does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No data is collected, transmitted to us, or shared with third parties.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Your data and sharing

Your pins, notes, elevation, and photos are stored locally on your device. Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to share a pin — the Share action produces a short sentence of seven words encoding the pin's coordinates and nothing else, which you send through an app of your choice. The pin's name, note and photos are not included.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete a pin or uninstall the app, which removes all associated data including photos.

Children

Loadstone is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
